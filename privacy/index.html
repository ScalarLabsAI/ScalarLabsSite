Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ScalarLabs, LLC may collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit the ScalarLabs website, contact ScalarLabs, apply for opportunities, or use ScalarLabs products and services, including Vocari.

1. Website Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with the ScalarLabs website, we may collect:

  • Contact information, such as name, email address, phone number, company, and message content.
  • Application information, such as resume materials or employment inquiry details.
  • Technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, referral source, and basic usage logs.
  • Product, partnership, research, or business inquiry information voluntarily provided through email or forms.

2. Vocari App Privacy

Vocari is designed with privacy as a core principle. ScalarLabs does not require users to create an account to use Vocari and does not collect or retain personal data from the Vocari app for advertising, analytics, profiling, or sale.

ScalarLabs does not collect or retain:

  • Vocari voice audio.
  • Live transcript content.
  • Session conversations.
  • User display names or profile photos for storage on ScalarLabs servers.
  • Advertising identifiers or behavioral analytics data.

Vocari does not currently use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs that collect user information on behalf of ScalarLabs.

3. Information Shared Between Vocari Users

Vocari enables direct communication between participating devices. Information such as voice audio, live transcript text, display names, profile images, session information, or other communication-related data may be transmitted between participants as necessary to provide Vocari's communication features.

This information is used to provide the communication session and is not collected or retained by ScalarLabs as part of the current Vocari service.

4. Device Permissions and Platform Services

Vocari may request access to device capabilities that are necessary for the app to function, including:

  • Microphone access for voice communication.
  • Bluetooth or connected audio-device access.
  • Local network or nearby-device access for communication between devices.
  • Camera access when scanning QR codes.
  • Photo access when a user chooses to select a profile image.
  • Speech recognition access for optional live transcription features.

These permissions are controlled by the operating system. Users may review or revoke them through their device privacy settings. Vocari may also rely on services provided by Apple, Google, or the device operating system. Use of those platform services may be subject to the applicable platform provider's privacy policies and settings.

5. How We Use Website Information

ScalarLabs may use information collected through the website to:

  • Respond to inquiries, requests, applications, and business communications.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the website.
  • Evaluate product, partnership, employment, or research opportunities.
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, or misuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

The ScalarLabs website may use basic cookies, server logs, or privacy-respecting analytics to understand site performance and usage. Website data practices are separate from the data practices of the Vocari app.

7. Affiliate Links and Third-Party Purchases

ScalarLabs websites or products may contain links to third-party products or services, including recommended earbuds, headsets, or related hardware. Some of these links may be affiliate links, meaning ScalarLabs may receive a commission if a user makes a qualifying purchase.

Purchases made through third-party websites are processed by those third parties. ScalarLabs does not control their payment systems, data collection, privacy practices, or terms. Users should review the privacy policies and terms of the applicable third-party provider before completing a purchase.

8. Information Sharing

ScalarLabs does not sell personal information. We may share website-related information with service providers who help operate the website or process communications, with professional advisors, or when required to comply with law, protect rights, prevent fraud, or secure systems.

ScalarLabs does not sell Vocari communication data or use Vocari voice audio, transcripts, profile information, or session content for advertising.

9. Data Retention

ScalarLabs retains website and business-contact information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, unless a longer period is required for legal, security, business, or recordkeeping reasons.

ScalarLabs does not currently retain Vocari voice audio, live transcript content, or session conversations on ScalarLabs servers.

10. Security

ScalarLabs uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No device, website, network, transmission method, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Choices

You may contact ScalarLabs to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have provided to us, subject to legal, security, and operational requirements.

Vocari users may control app permissions through their device settings and may choose whether to enable optional features such as transcription or profile images.

12. Children’s Privacy

ScalarLabs services are not specifically directed to children under 13. ScalarLabs does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the ScalarLabs website or the current Vocari app. If ScalarLabs becomes aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected in violation of applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

13. Third-Party Websites and Services

The ScalarLabs website and products may link to third-party websites or services. ScalarLabs is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security, or policies of third parties.

14. Changes to This Policy

ScalarLabs may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as products, services, legal requirements, or data practices change. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

15. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or ScalarLabs privacy practices may be sent through the ScalarLabs contact page.
